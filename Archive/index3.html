Welcome to Copson Street Dental Practice

Dentist in Withington, Manchester

Copson Street Dental is a family-focused mixed dental practice providing both NHS and private dental treatment. It’s located in Withington, a suburb of South Manchester.

We strive to be a caring practice providing high-quality treatments to our patients. We have an excellent team of dentists, dental nurses, and reception staff who all have a genuine concern for our patient’s best interest and wellbeing.
