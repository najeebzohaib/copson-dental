Welcome to Copson Street Dental Practice

Dentist in Withington, Manchester

Copson Street Dental is a family-focused mixed dental practice providing both NHS and private dental treatment. It’s located in Withington, a suburb of South Manchester. Our aim is to provide our patients with long term dental well being with an emphasis on prevention, providing dental care to suit their needs.

We strive to be a caring practice providing high-quality treatments to our patients. We have an excellent team of dentists, dental nurses, and reception staff who all have a genuine concern for our patient’s best interest and wellbeing and this is reflected in the care and treatment we provide.

Excellence in Dental Care

We will always take the time to discuss your treatment and answer any concerns ensuring the patient is confident and relaxed allowing us to provide quality dental care. New patients are welcome on an independent or Practice Plan basis. If you are not a registered patient, we are also able to offer individual visits – just call to arrange an appointment. At the moment, we are full to capacity and not able to accept new NHS patients. We can provide everything from teeth whitening and dental implants to oral hygiene services, and we’re proud to offer an exceptional standard of patient care. We offer a full range of dental treatments including professional cleaning, fillings, root canal treatment, periodontal treatment, crowns, onlays, veneers, extractions, dentures, and bridges.
