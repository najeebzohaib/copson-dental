Meet Our Professional Team

We have an excellent team of dentists, dental nurses, and reception staff who all have a genuine concern for our patient’s best interest and wellbeing.

Our Team

Shahid Muzaffar Chawoo

Principal Dentist

BDS, MFDS RCS Eng

GDC No. 170093

Shahid qualified in 2000 from University of Kashmir and in 2004 he came to UK for further education and training. He worked as a senior house officer in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery in different hospitals across UK and through examinations gained Membership of the Faculty of Dental Surgery of The Royal College of Surgeons of England and completed the statutory exam from GDC for practicing in the General Dental Practice.

Shahid started his career as a Senior House Officer in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery at Derbyshire Royal Infirmary. Seeing his dedication and enthusiasm for oral surgery he was promoted to Specialist Doctor in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery at University Hospitals of Derby and Burton NHS, performing oral surgical procedures and assisting in cancer and orthognathic surgeries. Shahid was also involved in the teaching of dental/medical graduates and senior house officers.

Shahid started working in General dental Practice since 2009. He’s completed multiple courses on implants, restorative dentistry and short-term orthodontics. Shahid has continued his keen interest in surgical aspects of dentistry and has been placing implants since 2014. He has attended multiple courses on Dental Implants and is currently pursuing the acclaimed Diploma in Implant Dentistry conducted by FGDP.

He has also completed Certificate Restorative courses by Prof Paul Tipton and attended short-term orthodontic courses for 6 months smile and C-fast. He is married with three children and when not working enjoys spending quality time with his family. Being an avid sports enthusiast Shahid enjoys playing sports especially football and cricket.

Inas Smeda Hassan Smeda

Dentist

BDS Tripoli University, Libya | MSc University of Manchester, UK | MFDS RCS Ed UK

GDC No. 313932

Inas completed her undergraduate studies in dentistry at the University of Tripoli. Following her graduation, she worked as a GDP in Al-Najah Dental Centre offering a range of general dentistry services to the patients.

She also worked as a clinical and academic tutor at the restorative department at the University of Tripoli and was granted a scholarship to undertake postgraduate education. Inas completed her Clinical Master's degree in Fixed and Removable Prosthodontics from the University of Manchester in 2020. During her training, she developed clinical skills in minimally invasive approaches to restorative treatment, completed full mouth rehabilitation cases and treated patients with complex restorative needs.

Inas has a special interest in fixed and removable prosthodontics (replacement of missing teeth), restoring worn or damaged teeth and smile makeovers using composite bonding, porcelain veneers and crowns. The ultimate reward for Inas is to see patients leave with a smile, knowing they feel confident and well cared for.

Inas wants to further her skills and knowledge with postgraduate courses and achieve to enable her to achieve best results for the patient. During her spare time, Inas enjoys going to the gym, spending quality time with. the family and sightseeing.
