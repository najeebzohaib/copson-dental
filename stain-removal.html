What is Airflow Stain Removal?

A revolutionary gentle and non-invasive dental treatment that utilizes to remove surface stains and dental plaque utilizing a precise mixture of water, fine powder particles, and compressed air to effectively remove unsightly stains caused by tea, coffee, tobacco, and certain foods. The result is a dazzling smile that radiates health and beauty.

Airflow treatment is a versatile and effective method that can be used repeatedly by both adults and children. It is highly recommended for removing biofilm, stains, and young calculus from natural teeth, restorations, and implants. It is also suitable for hypersensitive patients, orthodontic patients, and recall appointments.

This treatment is capable of reaching even the most difficult areas with precision. A single Airflow session can eliminate plaque and discoloration caused by daily habits. Moreover, Airflow treatment is considered the best solution for managing gum disease and maintaining oral health. It is also a convenient option for quickly improving your smile before special occasions like weddings, holidays, or job interviews.

Benefits of Airflow Treatment

  • Efficient Plaque Removal

    Airflow treatment has the unique advantage of rapidly and effectively eliminating heavy plaque build-up. This method outperforms the capabilities of traditional scaling and polishing.

  • Pain-Free

    Setting itself apart from various dental procedures, Airflow guarantees a pain-free experience, reducing the apprehension often associated with dental visits.

  • Stain Removal

    Beyond just plaque, the treatment is adept at removing stubborn stains, ensuring teeth are not just healthy but also aesthetically pleasing.

  • Reaches Deep Pockets

    The treatment has the prowess to penetrate deep into periodontal pockets, eliminating harmful bacteria that can escalate into major dental complications.

  • Preventive Care

    By effectively targeting harmful bacteria, it aids in the prevention of common dental ailments like caries, gingivitis, and periodontitis.

  • Enhances Whitening

    Those seeking to whiten their teeth will find Airflow treatment beneficial. It clears any build-up that could inhibit the bleaching process, ensuring optimal results.

  • Minimally Invasive

    With a focus on patient comfort, the procedure is designed to be minimally invasive, making the experience more pleasant for patients.

  • Adjustable Water Temperature

    Recognizing that many individuals have sensitive teeth, the treatment offers adjustable water temperatures, ensuring comfort during the procedure.

  • Child-Friendly

    Its non-intrusive nature makes it a suitable and effective dental solution for children, ensuring they have a positive dental experience.

  • Immediate Results

    The benefits aren’t just long-term. Patients can witness a distinctively brighter and healthier smile immediately after a single session.

  • Safe for Restorations

    Those with dental restorations can undergo the treatment without concerns. It is completely safe for veneers, crowns, bridges, and implants.
