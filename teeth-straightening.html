About SwiftFlex

At SwiftFlex, your smile is our priority. We believe everyone deserves a beautiful smile without sacrificing comfort or confidence.

Our virtually invisible, custom-made aligners discreetly straighten your teeth, allowing you to maintain your lifestyle. Enjoy the freedom to eat, drink, and maintain oral hygiene with ease, knowing our aligners blend seamlessly with your natural teeth.

We prioritise care and focus on making you smile every day. Our advanced technology ensures smooth and efficient treatment, while innovative materials minimise discomfort.

Treatable Cases

SwiftFlex Aligners can be used to treat a wide range of orthodontic issues.

Crowded Teeth

Too little room for teeth.

Gap Teeth

Too much room for teeth.

Overbite

Front teeth stick out.

Underbite

Lower jaw sticks out forward.

Crossbite

Upper teeth fit inside lower teeth.

Open Bite

Gap between teeth when biting down.

Crooked Teeth

Teeth are crooked or misaligned.

How Does SwiftFlex Work?

  • 1. Initial Assessment

    Your SwiftFlex-trained dentist will take photos, X-rays and digital scans to create your treatment plan.

  • 2. Customised Treatment Plan

    SwiftFlex uses these records to develop a customised 3D treatment plan, showing your expected results and treatment duration.

  • 3. Production of Aligners

    Custom aligners are produced and sent to your dentist.

  • 4. Receiving Aligners

    The dentist will decide how many aligners you will receive at a time. You will wear each set daily and change to a new set every 1-2 weeks.

  • 5. Regular Check-ups

    Your dentist will advise on the schedule and determine regular visits to monitor your progress.

  • 6. Post-Treatment Care

    After treatment, retainers may be needed to maintain your new smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are clear aligners?
Clear aligners are transparent, custom-made plastic trays designed to straighten teeth without traditional metal braces.
How do clear aligners work?
Aligners gradually shift teeth into their desired position by applying gentle, consistent pressure. They are replaced every 1-2 weeks with a new set to continue the process.
How long does the treatment take?
Treatment duration typically ranges from 6 to 18 months, depending on individual cases.
Can I eat and drink with my aligners on?
No, aligners should be removed before eating or drinking anything other than water to prevent staining and damage.
Are aligners painful?
Aligners are generally more comfortable than traditional braces, though some initial discomfort may occur when starting a new set. SwiftFlex's material selection significantly reduces this discomfort.
